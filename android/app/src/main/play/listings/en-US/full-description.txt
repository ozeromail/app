All your email. One calm inbox.

I'm making the mail app I couldn't find for myself. Ozero Mail brings Gmail, iCloud, Yandex, Exchange and any IMAP mailbox into one list and keeps it quiet.

ONE INBOX FOR EVERY ACCOUNT
• Inbox, Sent, Archive and Flagged are shared across accounts, like in Apple Mail.
• Each account gets a color dot and a name you choose. Fold the folders you don't need.
• Any folder of any account is a tap away.

PEOPLE FIRST
• Letters from people come first. Notifications and newsletters wait in their own tabs, sorted by simple rules right on your phone.

INSTANT
• Archive, move, delete and mark happen at once; the server catches up in the background. Undo is always there.
• Set what swipes do. Choose many letters with two fingers or a long press.
• Done means archived. Reach Inbox Zero and see a still lake.

MADE FOR READING
• A conversation on one page, with quotes folded.
• Inter for the interface, Literata for letters. Wide newsletters fit the screen.
• On a tablet or a phone on its side, mailboxes, list and letter sit side by side.

WRITING
• A full-screen editor. Drafts are saved as you type, and a send can be undone for 5 seconds.
• Reply, reply all, forward with attachments.
• Search finds mail on your phone and on the server.

PRIVATE
• No AI, no ads, no tracking.
• Mail goes from your provider straight to your phone; my servers never see it.
• Mail, passwords and settings stay on your phone, in an encrypted database.

This is a beta. I answer every letter myself: write to me right from the app.
